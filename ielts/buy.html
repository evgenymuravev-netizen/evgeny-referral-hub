What it is not

It does not predict your band, and it does not judge whether your ideas are any good — which is most of Task Response. It finds the mechanical faults that cost marks before an examiner gets that far. Clearing every flag is worth roughly half a band. The rest is on you, and eventually on a human teacher.

Questions

Is IELTS for UKVI different from normal IELTS?

Only administratively. Identical content, format, timing, marking and band scores. The differences are that it must be taken at a Home Office approved centre, the session is video-recorded, and the Test Report Form carries a UKVI number. So ordinary IELTS practice is exactly right — just make sure you book the UKVI version.

Academic or General Training?

Both are included and you switch with one click. Listening and Speaking are identical between them; only Reading and Writing differ. General Training is the usual route for spouse, family, settlement and many work visas; Academic is for degree-level study.
